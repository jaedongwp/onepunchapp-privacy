Privacy Policy

Knife Hit RPG, a game for Android by onepunchapp. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Knife Hit RPG collects no information about you. It has no account, shows no ads, sells nothing inside the game, sends nothing to us or to anyone else, and asks for no permissions on your phone.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and your settings, such as the chapters you have opened, the heroes and upgrades you have unlocked, your coins and the sound volume, in a file inside the game's own folder on your phone. That file never leaves your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The game uses no servers of ours, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it never reads your phone's advertising ID.

The Discord link

The start screen has a sign that opens our community's server on Discord. Tapping it opens the invite in your browser or in the Discord app; the game sends nothing with it. What you do on Discord is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Google Play downloads and updates the game under Google's Privacy Policy. Google shows us counts that name no one, such as how many people installed the game and, from phones whose owners chose to share it with Google, how often it crashed.

Deleting it

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes the save file. We hold nothing else to delete.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the game ever starts to collect anything, for example for purchases or ads, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
