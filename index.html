Privacy Policy

Emily's Only Friend, a game for Android by onepunchapp. Last updated 27 October 2026.

This page explains what Emily's Only Friend does with your information: what stays on your phone, what goes to Google and to Unity, and how to delete it. The game has no account of its own, shows no ads, and never sells your information or uses it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and your settings, such as the levels you have reached and the sound volume, in a file inside the game's own folder on your phone.

Your saved game in Google Play Games

When the game starts, it signs you in to Google Play Games, Google's games service on your phone, and keeps a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games saved games: the levels you have finished and what has happened in the story. That copy is what lets you carry on after an uninstall or on a new phone. Google keeps it with your Google account, under Google's Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it.

Purchases

The first levels are free, and the rest of the game is unlocked with one purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or payment details.

When you buy, the store software the game uses, made by Unity (the company behind the game's engine), sends Unity a record of the purchase: what was bought, its order number, the game's version, your phone's model, Android version, languages, storage size and when it was last switched on, and a random number that stands for this installation of the game. Unity handles it under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The demo: sharing how you play, only if you say yes

The demo of the game asks you once, the first time you open it, whether to share how you play. If you choose Share, the demo sends Unity Analytics:

  • each level you start and finish, how many seconds you spent in it, and how many times you died in it;
  • when you open and close the game;
  • your phone's model, Android version, processor, graphics, memory, screen size, battery level and volume, your language and country, and the game's version;
  • a random number that stands for this installation of the game, and a scrambled form of the ID Android gives this game on your phone, which stays the same if you reinstall.

It never sends your name, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID. We use it to see how long each level takes and where players stop, to make the game better. Unity handles it for us under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

If you choose Don't share, the demo sends nothing to Unity Analytics. You can change your answer any time in the demo's Settings, under Share how you play. The full game never asks and never sends this.

Keeping it safe

Everything the game sends goes over an encrypted connection. We see only what the demo shares, on Unity's dashboard; we never see your Google account or your saved game.

Keeping and deleting it

  • The save file on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you clear the game's storage in your phone's settings.
  • The copy in Google Play Games is cleared when you start the game over with Play again. You can also delete a game's saved data in Google Play Games, and Google keeps or deletes it as your Google account settings say.
  • What the demo shared: turn off Share how you play in the demo's Settings, which stops the sending and asks Unity to delete what your phone sent. You can also write to us at the address below and we will ask Unity to delete it.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google and by Unity under their own policies.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

When this changes, this page is updated, with a new date at the top.
