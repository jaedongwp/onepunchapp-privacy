Privacy Policy

Emily's Only Friend, a game for Android by onepunchapp. Last updated 4 October 2026.

This page explains what Emily's Only Friend does with your information. The game does not collect, send or share any personal or sensitive information.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no account and no sign-in. It shows no ads and has no analytics or crash reporting. It plays offline, and it sends nothing about you or your phone to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and your settings, such as the levels you have reached and the sound volume, in a file inside the game's own folder on your phone. That file never leaves your phone and we cannot see it.

Purchases

The first levels are free, and the rest of the game is unlocked with one purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or payment details.

Keeping it safe

Because the game sends nothing, none of your information travels over the internet from the game and none of it is stored on our side.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your save file is deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you clear the game's storage in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
