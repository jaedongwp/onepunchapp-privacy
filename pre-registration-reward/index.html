Pre-registration Reward

Emily's Only Friend, a game for Android by onepunchapp. Last updated 7 October 2026.

These terms cover the reward for pre-registering for Emily's Only Friend on Google Play. They apply when you pre-register.

The reward

Emily's tin lantern: a black tin lantern with tiny holes and a big star cut into it, which Emily carries in the game in place of her usual one. It looks different and works the same. It gives no advantage and adds no levels or story.

Who receives it

Everyone who pre-registers on Google Play before the game is released, in a country or region where pre-registration is open. One lantern per Google account.

How you receive it

When the game is released, Google Play adds the lantern to the Google account you pre-registered with, free. Install and open the game on a phone signed in to that account, and the game gives Emily the lantern and tells you it has arrived.

Good to know

The lantern has no cash value. It can't be exchanged for money, sold, or moved to another Google account.

Google Play

Pre-registration and the delivery of the reward are run by Google Play, under the Google Play Terms of Service.

Changes

If these terms ever change, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
