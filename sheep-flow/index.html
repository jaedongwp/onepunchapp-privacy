Privacy Policy

Sheep Flow, a game for Android by onepunchapp. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Sheep Flow has no account and collects nothing about you. This version of the game does not use the internet at all.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and your settings, such as your farm, your upgrades, your helpers, your money and the vibration setting, in a file inside the game's own folder on your phone. That file never leaves your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The game uses no servers of ours, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code, and it never reads your phone's advertising ID.

What the game asks of your phone

The only permission the game asks for is to vibrate the phone, for its haptic feedback. You can turn that off in the game's settings.

Google Play

Google Play downloads and updates the game under Google's Privacy Policy. Google shows us counts that name no one, such as how many people installed the game and, from phones whose owners chose to share it with Google, how often it crashed.

Deleting it

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes the save file.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

When this changes, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
